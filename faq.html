NEED TO KNOW

FREQUENTLY ASKED.

A few answers before you find your next favourite piece.

Are all the clothes second-hand? +

Yes. Onism Thrifting focuses on pre-loved clothing and accessories. Every item is individually checked before being listed.

How do I know the condition of an item? +

Each product includes a condition description and clear photographs. Any noticeable flaws or signs of wear will be mentioned in the product description.

Are the items one-of-a-kind? +

Most pieces are unique or available in very limited quantities. Once a one-of-one item is sold, it may not return.

How do I know what size an item is? +

Every product page includes the listed size and, where applicable, garment measurements to help you judge the fit.

How does payment work? +

Purchases are completed securely through our payment platform at checkout. Available payment options will be shown during checkout.

Do you offer delivery? +

Delivery options and applicable costs will be displayed during checkout. Collection options may also be available depending on the arrangement.

Can I return an item? +

Our returns policy will be displayed before checkout and should be reviewed before placing an order.

Can I donate my clothes? +

Absolutely. Visit our Donate page and submit your details. We'll review the items and contact you about the next steps.
